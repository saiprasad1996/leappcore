LEAPP Partners

Partner Guidelines

These guidelines help keep LEAPP a trusted place for families and educators. By applying for verification, you agree to follow them.

verified_user Be accurate and honest

  • Share truthful details about your experience, qualifications, and offerings.
  • Only upload documents that belong to you or your organisation.
  • Keep your profile and course information up to date.

family_restroom Put families first

  • Describe offerings clearly so parents know what to expect.
  • Respond to interest and messages in a timely, respectful way.
  • Never pressure families or share their private information.

school Keep learning experiences safe

  • Follow age-appropriate practices and local child-safety expectations.
  • Do not promote harmful, discriminatory, or illegal content.
  • Report issues that put learners at risk to LEAPP support promptly.

handshake Work in good faith with LEAPP

  • Verification is a trust signal — it does not guarantee bookings or ranking.
  • LEAPP may request updates or revoke verification if guidelines are not followed.
  • Contact support if your situation changes or you need help with your listing.
Questions? Reach out through the contact options on the LEAPP website. We’re here to help partners succeed.
